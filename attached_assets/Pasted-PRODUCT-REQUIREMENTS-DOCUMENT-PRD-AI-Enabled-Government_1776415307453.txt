PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
AI-Enabled Government HR Recruitment & Workforce Management Portal
1. Product Overview

The proposed system is a fully integrated Human Resource Recruitment and Workforce Management Portal designed to digitise, automate, and optimise the entire employee lifecycle—from job advertisement to contract expiry and workforce planning.

Initially deployed at the PNG National Institute of Standards and Industrial Technology, the system will be built as a multi-tenant, scalable platform that can be adopted by other government agencies across Papua New Guinea.

The platform will leverage Artificial Intelligence (AI) to enhance recruitment efficiency, decision-making, and workforce planning.

2. Objectives
Digitise and standardise recruitment processes
Improve transparency and accountability in hiring
Enable real-time workforce visibility and planning
Reduce administrative overhead through automation
Provide predictive insights using AI
Create a reusable government-wide HR platform

4. Core Modules
4.1 Recruitment Management Module

Features:

Job requisition creation and approval workflow
Vacancy publishing (public portal)
Application intake and storage
Shortlisting tools

AI Enhancements:

CV parsing and structured data extraction
AI-based candidate ranking (skills, experience match)
Automated screening recommendations
4.2 Applicant Tracking System (ATS)

Features:

Candidate pipeline (Applied → Shortlisted → Interview → Selected → Rejected)
Interview scheduling
Notes and evaluation scoring

AI Enhancements:

Interview question generation based on job role
Sentiment analysis (optional for interview notes)
Candidate fit scoring
4.3 Employee & Workforce Management Module

Features:

Employee master records
Contract lifecycle tracking
Department and position mapping
Staff deployment visibility

AI Enhancements:

Predictive contract expiry alerts
Workforce gap analysis
Attrition risk prediction
4.4 Contract Management Module

Features:

Contract creation and storage
Expiry tracking and alerts
Renewal workflows

AI Enhancements:

Automated renewal recommendations

4.5 Analytics & Decision Support Dashboard

Features:

Vacancy vs filled positions

Predictive hiring needs
